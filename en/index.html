Receipt OCR

Problem
Company name, tax ID, date, VAT and totals of hundreds of receipts were typed in by hand for bookkeeping.

Sample project · 2026

Cari Takip: Receivables & Installment Tracker

Problem
Small businesses that sell on credit or in installments keep receivables in a ledger or in Excel, which makes overdue payments hard to spot and chase.
Solution
A desktop program that works offline: the installment plan is previewed before saving, partial payments are recorded, and a ready-made WhatsApp reminder opens for overdue customers. Amounts are stored as integer kuruş; the rounding remainder goes to the last installment and due dates follow month ends (31 Jan, 28 Feb, 31 Mar).
Result
Runs as a single .exe and the data stays on the user's computer. The business rules are separate from the UI and verified by 49 automated tests.

Tool · 2026

Scanned PDF → Excel

Problem
Tables in scanned PDFs can't be copied, and Turkish number format (1.250,50) stays as text in most converters, so Excel can't sum it.
Solution
Pages with a text layer are read directly, scanned pages with OCR; columns are found from whitespace corridors on the page. Web (drag and drop) and desktop interfaces share one core.
Result
The sample document was converted in 1.2 seconds; numbers are written as real numbers, so formulas work immediately.
